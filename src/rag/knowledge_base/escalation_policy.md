# Incident Escalation Policy

## Normal

No significant anomaly evidence.

Action:
- Continue routine monitoring.

## Low

Limited anomaly evidence affecting one sensor.

Action:
- Continue monitoring.
- Investigate if the condition persists.

## Medium

Multiple sensor signals or moderate anomaly evidence.

Action:
- Increase monitoring.
- Request additional diagnostic evidence.

## High

Strong anomaly evidence or significant sensor abnormalities.

Action:
- Escalate for operational assessment.
- Request additional diagnostic evidence.
- Notify maintenance personnel when appropriate.

## Critical

Very strong anomaly evidence or multiple severe indicators.

Action:
- Immediately escalate for operational assessment.
- Request additional diagnostic evidence.
- Prioritize maintenance intervention.
- Do not automatically perform physical shutdown without authorized operational confirmation.

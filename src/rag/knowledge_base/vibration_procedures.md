# Vibration Inspection Procedure

When abnormal vibration is detected:

1. Confirm that the vibration signal persists.
2. Inspect the pump and rotating equipment.
3. Check mounting bolts and structural supports.
4. Check shaft and coupling alignment.
5. Inspect bearings for visible damage or unusual conditions.
6. Record inspection observations.
7. Escalate persistent or severe abnormalities to maintenance personnel.

Do not assume a specific component has failed without supporting evidence.

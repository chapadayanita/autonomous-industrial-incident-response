# Pump Fault Diagnosis

## Mechanical Vibration

Abnormal vibration in rotating equipment can indicate mechanical problems.

Possible causes include:
- Bearing degradation
- Shaft misalignment
- Rotor imbalance
- Loose mounting
- Coupling problems

Recommended inspection:
1. Inspect bearings and rotating components.
2. Check shaft and coupling alignment.
3. Inspect equipment mounting.
4. Check for unusual mechanical noise.
5. Compare vibration with normal operating conditions.

## Pressure Anomalies

Unexpected pressure changes may indicate:
- Leakage
- Blockage
- Valve problems
- Pump performance degradation

Recommended inspection:
1. Check pressure readings.
2. Inspect connected pipes and valves.
3. Check for visible leakage.
4. Verify pump operating conditions.
